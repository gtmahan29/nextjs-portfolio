## Shared direction

Product mockup for a premium design studio portfolio. Use the supplied project interface as the exact UI/content reference, not the target composition. Integrate that interface into the device screen, retaining its recognizable design, language, dominant colours and layout. Single editorial photograph/render, readable device, tangible materials, sharp screen, correct perspective, grounded contact shadows, balanced negative space and full-bleed background. No external headings, collage grid, portfolio navigation, watermarks, people, hands or added logos outside the screen.
